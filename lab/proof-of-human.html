Forensic record

Written by Rober González, in Spain. Set in GT Super Display, with Baskerville (Georgia on Windows) standing in for the few marks the display cut lacks, and in your system’s own monospace.

The numbered notes in the margins are an apparatus, derived in your browser from the keystroke tape embedded in this page: each gives the final reading, a bracket, and the text it replaced. Typos fixed on the fly live only on the tape. Drag the tape, or focus it and use the arrow keys; press Esc or click the page to jump back to the final text.

This page carries a keystroke tape of its own writing. Replaying it, and the apparatus of edits drawn from it, need JavaScript; what you are reading is the final text.
