Four other ways to say hi.

The lab is where I take my home page apart and build it again. Same words, same facts — told another way each time.

Every experiment is a single HTML file: vanilla CSS and JavaScript, self-hosted fonts and no third-party requests. Two are nearly there; the rest are sketches.

  1. 01 · Finalist

    Proof of Humanlab / proof-of-human

    My bio as a manuscript you can scrub back through, keystroke by keystroke, with every crossed-out word kept in the margin. It comes with a tape recorder, so the real take gets typed by me.

  2. 02 · Finalist

    The Chart Is the Biolab / dataviz

    My career as one editorial chart, drawn to scale from the dates on my CV, next to a portrait made of about four thousand dots that scatter as you move through them.

  3. 03 · Sketch

    Colour Referencelab / wildcard

    A paper fan deck whose palette is pulled from my portrait, live in your browser, much like my Figma plugin does with designs.

  4. 04 · Sketch

    Open on the Canvaslab / canvas

    The home as a design file you can pan and zoom, with frames, sticky notes and a layers panel for getting around.

Got an opinion on any of them? Tell me by Email, or head back to the page they’re all trying to replace.
