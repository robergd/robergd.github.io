Tape recorder

Type the manuscript yourself, block by block: the title, then each paragraph. Enter moves to the next block. Revise the way you normally would; every insertion, deletion, dead key and caret move is recorded with its timing. Pastes are recorded and flagged, because pasted text is not proof.

Nothing leaves this browser. The recording is kept here (in this browser’s storage) until you export it.

00:00.0 · 0 keys · 0 deleted · 0 pasted

Events will appear here as you type.

Variants

Derived from the tape exactly as the page will derive them. Typos fixed on the fly stay on the tape only. Add a short note to any variant, change how many words its reading spans, or leave it out of the apparatus.
